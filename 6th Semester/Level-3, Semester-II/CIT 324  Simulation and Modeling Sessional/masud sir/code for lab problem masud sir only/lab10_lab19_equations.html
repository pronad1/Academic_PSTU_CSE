📐 Equations
Every formula used in the simulation logic.
1 · Exponential Failure — PDF
$$f(t;\lambda)=\lambda e^{-\lambda t}, \quad t\ge 0$$
Failure rate λ = 1/50 = 0.02 h⁻¹
2 · Exponential Failure — CDF
$$F(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}$$
Probability of failing by time t
3 · Exponential — Mean & Variance
$$\mu=\frac{1}{\lambda}=50\;\text{h},\quad \sigma^2=\frac{1}{\lambda^2}=2500$$
Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)
4 · Inverse-Transform Sampling
$$T_{\text{fail}}=-\mu\ln(U),\quad U\sim\mathcal{U}(0,1)$$
Generates np.random.exponential(50)
5 · Weibull Repair — PDF
$$f(t;k,\lambda)=\frac{k}{\lambda}\!\left(\frac{t}{\lambda}\right)^{k-1}e^{-(t/\lambda)^k}$$
Shape k=2, scale λ=10
6 · Weibull Repair — CDF
$$F(t;k,\lambda)=1-e^{-(t/\lambda)^k}$$
Probability repair is done by time t
7 · Weibull — Mean Repair Time
$$\mathbb{E}[T_{\text{repair}}]=\lambda\,\Gamma\!\left(1+\frac{1}{k}\right)$$
= 10·Γ(1.5) = 10·0.8862 ≈ 8.86 h
8 · System Availability
$$A = \frac{\text{Total Uptime}}{N \times T_{\text{sim}}} \times 100\%$$
N=5 machines, T=1000 h
9 · Total Throughput
$$Q = \text{Total Uptime} \times r_p$$
Production rate r_p = 10 units/h/machine
10 · Total Downtime
$$D = N \cdot T_{\text{sim}} - \text{Total Uptime}$$
Machine-hours lost to failures
11 · Theoretical Availability
$$A_{\text{th}}=\frac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF}+\text{MTTR}}=\frac{50}{50+8.86}\approx 84.9\%$$
Single-machine theoretical benchmark
12 · Weibull Failure Rate (hazard)
$$h(t)=\frac{k}{\lambda}\left(\frac{t}{\lambda}\right)^{k-1}$$
Increasing hazard for k=2 > 1 (wear-out)

📐 Equations
Every formula used in the ER simulation.
1 · Poisson Arrival Process — PDF
$$f(t;\lambda_a)=\lambda_a e^{-\lambda_a t},\quad \lambda_a=\tfrac{1}{10}\,\text{min}^{-1}$$
Inter-arrival times: Exp(10 min)
2 · Triage Service Time — Uniform
$$T_{\text{triage}}\sim\mathcal{U}(a,b),\quad a=2,\;b=5\;\text{min}$$
E[T] = (a+b)/2 = 3.5 min
3 · Uniform PDF
$$f(t;a,b)=\frac{1}{b-a}, \quad a\le t\le b$$
Constant density over the support interval
4 · Doctor Treatment — Exponential (High)
$$T_{\text{treat}}^{(1)}\sim\text{Exp}(\mu_1),\quad \mu_1=45\;\text{min}$$
Critical patients — longest treatment
5 · Doctor Treatment — Exponential (Med & Low)
$$T_{\text{treat}}^{(2)}\sim\text{Exp}(20),\quad T_{\text{treat}}^{(3)}\sim\text{Exp}(10)$$
Urgent and Non-Urgent treatment times
6 · Patient Priority Assignment
$$P(U)=\begin{cases}1 & \text{if } U<0.2\\2 & \text{if } 0.2\le U<0.7\\3 & \text{if } U\ge 0.7\end{cases}$$
U ~ Uniform(0,1)
7 · Waiting Time per Patient
$$W_i = t_{\text{service start},i} - t_{\text{queue enter},i}$$
Time spent waiting for a doctor
8 · Average Wait by Priority
$$\bar{W}_p=\frac{1}{n_p}\sum_{i=1}^{n_p} W_i^{(p)},\quad p\in\{1,2,3\}$$
Mean doctor-wait time per priority class
9 · Nurse Start Time
$$t_{\text{start}} = \max(t_{\text{arrival}},\; t_{\text{nurse free}})$$
Patient waits if all nurses are busy
10 · Doctor Service Start
$$t_{\text{doc}} = \max(t_{\text{clock}},\; \min_j\{t_{\text{doc}_j\text{ free}}\})$$
Earliest available doctor is assigned
11 · Little's Law (theoretical)
$$L = \lambda_{\text{eff}} \cdot W$$
Average queue length = arrival rate × avg wait
12 · Expected Arrivals (48 h)
$$\mathbb{E}[N]= \lambda_a \cdot T_{\text{sim}} = \frac{1}{10}\times2880=288$$
Expected total patients in simulation
